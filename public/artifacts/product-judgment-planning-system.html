Scope protection rules
Anti-drift system — these rules are mandatory throughout the build
01
Assessment first
Landing page, AI comparisons, and branding must never become larger than the assessment itself.
02
No fake depth
No theory-heavy content, jargon, or inflated result language to make the product feel "smart."
03
No drift into content site
This is an assessment, not a blog, media site, or AI tool review.
04
No heavy auth in v1
No login-first, dashboard-first, or profile-heavy flows unless re-approved.
05
No overbuilding
Short public experiment, not a full platform. Speed and clarity over feature count.
06
Truthfulness wins
If there is ever a conflict between broader/prettier/louder and more truthful — choose truthful.
